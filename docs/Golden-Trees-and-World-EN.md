# Golden Trees, new building blocks and world generation

The 26.3 module adds a **Golden Tree** system with rare worldgen, hanging leaves, golden flowers/petals and golden saplings. It includes a full family of golden wood (logs, stripped wood, planks, stairs, slabs, fences, fence gates, doors, trapdoors, buttons, pressure plates, signs, hanging signs, ladders, shelves, torches and boats with and without chests).

The JAR contains `data/golems_materiaux/worldgen/feature/rare_golden_trees.json` and a corresponding placed feature, as well as Java generation and habitat logic. This confirms an implemented generation system, **not a verified measured spawn frequency**.

## Connected gameplay

Golden flowers power the **Healing Standard** (64-flower supply, roughly 8-block healing area advertised). Golden petals help craft Golden Elixir. Trees have environmental and creature interactions. Custom crafting JSON is listed in [all recipes](Recipes-EN.md).
